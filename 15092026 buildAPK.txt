I added the sleep quality (คุณภาพการนอน) module and hooked it into every screen that shows data from the other modules. flutter analyze finds no issues, flutter test passes all 86 tests (60 before, 26 new), and the release APK builds (54.7MB). I haven't run it on a real phone or emulator yet.

What the new screen does (lib/screens/sleep_screen.dart)

Open it from the full-width card on the home screen.
- Recording a night:
  - Bedtime and wake time, with duration worked out across midnight automatically (23:00 → 06:30 = 7 hrs 30 min).
  - How it felt: แย่ / พอใช้ / ดี / ดีมาก, plus how many times you woke in the night and a note.
  - ปัจจัยรบกวน (disturbances): caffeine, late meal, screens before bed, alcohol, stress, noise/light, late exercise. You can tick several.
  - One entry per night. Saving the same night again replaces the old entry, and tapping an entry opens it for editing or deletion (deletion asks first).
- Top card: last night's duration, the % of your target as a ring, bedtime, wake time and quality.
- Chart of the last 7 nights: each bar's colour shows that night's score.
- 7-night averages: sleep time, quality, and ความสม่ำเสมอของเวลาเข้านอน (how consistent your bedtime is).
- Tips from your own data, for example "sleeping 45 min less than target on average", waking often in the night, an irregular bedtime, or disturbances that keep coming up.

Nightly score is the average of three parts: sleep time against the target, the quality rating, and night wakings (−15% each, never below 40%).

Where it connects to the rest of the app

- Home screen: new sleep card (last night's hours, quality, score).
- Progress screen: sleep added to the overall score and bar chart, plus a "nights recorded" badge.
- Goal settings: new sleep target per night, entered in hours (default 8).
- Schedule: "คุณภาพการนอน" is now a category you can pick. A "เตรียมตัวเข้านอน" event will get the popup and alarm sound from the previous round.
- Backup/restore: sleep records are included in backup files and are wiped by "ล้างประวัติทั้งหมด".
- Sample data: a fresh install gets 7 nights of sleep records plus two sleep events in the schedule.

Also worth knowing

- One thing that's easy to get wrong in the bedtime-consistency calculation: a bedtime after midnight (01:00) has 24 hrs added before averaging. Otherwise 23:00 and 01:00 would count as 22 hours apart instead of 2. There's a test covering this.

- I updated README, HANDOFF and DATA_MODEL. HANDOFF also lists next ideas, such as an automatic "time for bed" reminder based on the target and wake time.
